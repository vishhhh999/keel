# Code of conduct

Keel is built for streamers, crews and the people who help them. Treat everyone here the way you would want your chat to treat you.

## Expected

- Be direct and kind. Critique the code, the design or the idea, never the person.
- Assume good intent. Ask before concluding.
- Share what you tested and how. Numbers beat adjectives.
- Respect people's time: search existing issues before opening a new one.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in any language.
- Sexual content or advances.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including stream keys, tokens, addresses or real names they have not shared.
- Raiding, brigading or pulling stream drama into this repository.
- Spam or self-promotion unrelated to Keel.

## Enforcement

Report problems to work@visheshmahendru.com. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept private. Depending on what happened, the response ranges from a warning to removing content to a permanent ban from the repository.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone represents the project.
