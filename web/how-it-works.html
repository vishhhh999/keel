How it works

Phones in. Platforms out. Keel in the middle.

Five parts, each boring on purpose. Everything above the waterline can fail. Everything below it keeps broadcasting.

01 Ingest

Two encoders.

Primary and backup phones send SRT, SRTLA or RTMP to mediamtx on secret paths.

02 Decide

The controller.

Reads bitrate, packet loss, late packets and RTT every second. Picks the healthier source. Degrades fast, recovers slow.

03 Compose

Headless OBS.

Scenes LIVE, LOW SIGNAL and BRB, your overlays. Encodes one program.

04 Relay

The shield.

Copies the program to every platform. Covers OBS crashes with BRB. Reconnects each platform alone.

05 Platforms

Steady out.

Twitch, Kick, YouTube, any RTMP or SRT target. They never see a phone drop.

The phones and the network are out of anyone's control. Ingest, decision, OBS and relay run in the cloud, next to the platforms.

The switching rule

Degrade fast. Recover slow.

InputConditionHolds forResult
Good800 kbps or more, packets arriving clean5 s to recoverLive.
Weak50 to 800 kbps, or over 2% late packets, or over 20% loss (SRT)3 s to degradeWeak signal.
Deadunder 50 kbps or disconnected2 s to degradeHolding on.

Every number is a default. Streamers change them live from the dashboard. With a backup encoder, the rule runs for each source and viewers see whichever is healthier.

Under the hood

Plain parts. Few of them.

mediamtx. The ingest server. SRT, RTMP and SRTLA through a receiver. Exposes bitrate and SRT link statistics.
Keel controller. Node 22, TypeScript, one dependency. 76 automated tests. Dashboard, API, chat bot, alerts, relay supervisor.
OBS Studio. Headless, driven over obs-websocket. Scenes are built automatically on first start.
Relay. One FFmpeg process per destination, copying without re-encoding, supervised with backoff.
Platform. Creates one isolated server per streamer, gives it a hostname with HTTPS, checks it is healthy, meters usage, and stops it when idle.

The signal moves. The stream holds. A reliability layer for people who stream from the street.
