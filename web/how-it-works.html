How it works

Phone in. Platform out. Keel in the middle.

Four parts. Each is boring on purpose. The only clever bit is the switching rule.

01 Ingest

Your phone sends SRT.

mediamtx receives SRT, RTMP or bonded SRTLA and exposes live bitrate.

02 Decide

The controller watches.

Polls bitrate every second. Degrades fast, recovers slow. Drives OBS over websocket.

03 Compose

Headless OBS.

Scenes LIVE, LOW SIGNAL and BRB. Renders and encodes in the cloud.

04 Broadcast

One steady stream out.

RTMP to Twitch, Kick and YouTube. The platform never sees your phone drop.

Everything above the line can fail. Everything below it keeps broadcasting.

The switching rule

Degrade fast. Recover slow.

InputConditionHolds forResult
Good800 kbps or more5 s to recoverLive.
Weak50 to 800 kbps3 s to degradeWeak signal.
Deadunder 50 kbps2 s to degradeHolding on.

All thresholds are configurable per streamer. Defaults come from the reference controller in controller/src/switcher.ts.

Also in the box

Operator tools.

Dashboard and API. Bearer token, live updates over SSE, history, Prometheus metrics, health check.
Chat commands. Moderators on Twitch can switch scenes or force BRB.
Reliability record. Every state change is logged, so you can show a streamer their uptime.
Idle shutdown. The server stops itself when nobody streams, so you do not pay for empty hours.
Mobile app. A companion app to watch state and switch scenes from the street.

The signal moves. The stream holds.
