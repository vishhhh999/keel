Phone setup

From pocket to platform in five minutes.

Open your Keel dashboard, pick Primary or Backup, and copy or scan the address. These are starting points. Every city and carrier is different, so do a test walk first.

Moblin (iPhone)

  1. Settings, Streams, add a stream.
  2. Paste the SRT or SRTLA address from your Keel dashboard as the URL.
  3. Video: H.264 or H.265, 1080p30, 4 to 6 Mbps. Enable adaptive bitrate.
  4. SRT latency around 2000 ms on cellular, lower on good Wi-Fi.

Bonding: use the SRTLA address and turn on cellular plus Wi-Fi.

IRL Pro (Android)

  1. Add a new connection, type SRT or SRTLA.
  2. Paste the address from the dashboard, or scan its QR code.
  3. Bitrate 4 to 6 Mbps, adaptive on.
  4. Latency 2000 ms to start, tune after a test walk.

Bonding: add every network interface and choose SRTLA.

Larix Broadcaster

  1. Settings, Connections, new connection.
  2. Paste the SRT address. Mode: Caller.
  3. Latency 2000 ms, max bandwidth relative to bitrate.
  4. Use the backup address on a second phone for failover.

RTMP works too, but SRT reports link quality to Keel.

Belabox

  1. In the Belabox UI, set the relay server to your Keel host.
  2. Port 5000, stream id from the dashboard's SRTLA address.
  3. Plug in your modems and Wi-Fi.
  4. Set Keel's backup address on a second unit or phone if you have one.

SRTLA receiver image is written but not yet field tested.

Field tips

Set it once. Walk with confidence.

Use SRT, not RTMP. SRT recovers lost packets and tells Keel how the link is doing. RTMP only reports bitrate.
Run a backup. A second phone on a different carrier, pointed at the backup address, turns most drops into a seamless switch.
Latency is a trade. Higher SRT latency rides out more bad network. 1500 to 2500 ms suits most cellular. Watch "Dropped" on the dashboard: if it rises, raise latency.
Tune the rules on day one. If you see BRB when the stream looked fine, lower "Gone below" or raise "BRB after" from the dashboard while live.

The signal moves. The stream holds. A reliability layer for people who stream from the street.
