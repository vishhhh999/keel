Where value is

The code is not the moat.

OBS, mediamtx and SRTLA are open source. Anyone can assemble them. What is hard to copy: proven reliability on real streets, a product that designs for failure first, and distribution through streamers with large, loyal audiences who switch tools when a peer they trust does.

The plan: one anchor streamer on a revenue share, a published drive-test video with real numbers, a reliability record on every stream, then their network of streamers.

Risks, stated plainly

What could go wrong.

Reliability is unproven in the field.

Containers are not a drive test. Days 2 and 3 exist for this, and nothing will be claimed before them.

Platform policy.

Simultaneous streaming and restreaming rules differ by platform and by contract. Review before selling multistream.

Incumbent response.

A larger toolkit can copy features. Reliability, design and an anchor streamer's network are the defence.

Team.

The author is a designer. Operating this needs an engineer on call during peak streaming hours.
