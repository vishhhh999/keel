Hosted streaming reliability is a subscription business.

Streamers pay monthly to avoid failing in front of their audience. The product is built and the core path is tested. What remains is field testing, a billing layer and distribution. A funded team can run that in a week.

What could go wrong.

Reliability claim is unproven in the field.

Container tests are not a drive test. Day 2 and 3 exist for this.

Platform policy.

Restream rules differ by platform. Review before selling.

Incumbent response.

A larger toolkit can copy features. Reliability and one anchor streamer are the defence.

GPU encoding.

NVENC path is untested. CPU x264 is the safe default.
