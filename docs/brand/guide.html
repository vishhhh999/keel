A calm, level line under a moving stream. Everything in this guide follows from that one idea.
01 · The idea

The signal moves.
The stream holds.

A streamer walks through a city. Networks wobble, phones overheat, trains go underground. Keel is the part that does not move: the fixed line under the boat.
Above the line

The signal moves.

Cellular, Wi-Fi, bonding links, a phone in a pocket. Out of anyone's control.

Below the line

The stream holds.

Ingest, encoder and platform connection stay up. Viewers see a designed screen, not a dead stream.

02 · Logo

One mark, level.

Primary lockup. Default on hull, buoy and bone. Mark only when the name sits nearby. Clear space is the height of the circle's bottom half on every side. Minimum width 96 px, mark only 24 px.
Never
Do not rotate the whole markDo not level the top halfDo not recolorDo not use low contrast

05 · Shape and layout

Sit on the line.

Waterline at 64% of the height
Domes stand on the line. Bowls hang from it.
Dome
Good news, live state, stats
Bowl
Weight, proof, records
Capsule
Bars, portraits, handles
Disc
Numbers, dots, status
Canvas 1920 by 1080. Margins 120, eyebrow at 84, title at 124, footer at 1004. Cards radius 36 to 44, outline 3 px or solid. Rounded, never sharp. No gradients, no shadows, no glow.
Brand guidelines06 / 10
06 · State

The tilt tells you.

Live.

Healthy bitrate. The top half sits almost level.

Mint on dark · Kelp on bone · 6° tilt
Weak signal.

Bitrate below the floor. A slow tilt, not an alarm.

Lamp · 12° tilt
Holding on.

Feed gone. Viewers see the BRB screen, broadcast stays up.

Buoy · 18° tilt
State is never color alone: a word, a color and a tilt angle always travel together, so it reads in sunlight and for color-blind users.
